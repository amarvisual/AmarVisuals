## 🏗️ 5. Key Architecture & Design Details

- **Scroll Animation Engine:** 300 sequential high-res JPEG frames rendered via HTML5 `<canvas>` using `object-fit: cover` math to fill the screen on all device resolutions.
- **Scroll Sync:** Canvas stays in fixed background, while content cards scroll over the animation simultaneously.
- **Design Language:** Luxury Dark Mode (`#0a0a0a`), Vibrant Orange Gradient (`#ff4500` → `#ff7a00`), Gold accents (`#c9a84c`), and frosted glass cards (`backdrop-filter: blur(18px)`).
- **Typography:** Google Fonts (`Inter`, `Outfit`, `Playfair Display`).
- **Interactive FX:** Custom magnetic cursor, 3D card tilt effect, particle ambient glow, counting stat numbers, infinite tech stack marquee.
- **Lead Capture:** Contact form wired directly to WhatsApp (`https://wa.me/916372885071`) with auto-generated message summaries.
- **Analytics & SEO:** Google Analytics 4 (`G-0EXSTST86E`), LocalBusiness JSON-LD Schema, Open Graph tags, canonical URLs, and XML sitemap.
