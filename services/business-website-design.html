Yes! Additional pages are available at ₹1,000 per page. For larger websites (10+ pages), consider our Premium Package at ₹19,999 which includes advanced SEO, speed optimization and 3 months of support.

We set up basic on-page SEO for all pages — proper title tags, meta descriptions, heading structure and image alt text. This gives your website the best chance of ranking on Google. For stronger rankings, we recommend also setting up Google Business Profile and submitting your sitemap to Google Search Console.
