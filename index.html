FAQ

Common Questions

How long does it take to build a website?+
Depending on the package, our standard turnaround time is 3 to 7 days. We prioritize quality while ensuring your business gets online quickly.
Do you provide domain and hosting?+
Domain and hosting are charged separately. We can help you purchase them, but keeping them in your name ensures you have 100% ownership of your assets.
What if I want to make changes later?+
All our packages include a set number of revisions during the build process. We also provide free support (1 to 3 months) after launch for any minor adjustments!
Do I need to pay everything upfront?+
No. We typically ask for a 50% deposit to start the project, and the remaining 50% is only paid once the website is complete and you are 100% satisfied.
